Audit Day Justification (ADJ) for ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and/or ISO 45001 

Version 2.2 
Intent of Use: 

Auditors shall use the present file to calculate audit days for the upcoming Certification Cycle (i.e. CR & SVs visits), or in case any interim changes have taken place, whereas 
Sales colleagues need to use this for any new Opportunity (i.e. IA, CTA, ToA) for certifications on our Core products (i.e. ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and/or ISO 45001). 

The purpose of this is to ensure that the correct information needed is collected and validated: 
-for the completion of the Technical Review, 
-the provision of proper justification for Accreditation purposes, 
-to have realistic and correct information in our systems, and 
-to update the contract(s) between LRQA and the clients. 

Therefore, it is crucial that Sales enter the information as provided by the client while Auditors confirm this information with the clients, as a minimum, during the Focus Visit. 

Using the file: 

For your convenience, start entering information in the sheets following from left to right tabs. All information in 'Client Info' sheet shall be completed in full in order for the 
other sheets to be filled in automatically. 
In the 'Client Info' tab you shall enter the information applicable to the Client (i.e. applicable Products, Codes, ), including all Sites (e.g. site name, address, whatever will 
distinguish the site), marking the applicable Standards with 'Yes' in the corresponding columns. 

Clarifications: The cells that cannot be modified are greyed out, whereas the cells that need your input are color tinted. 
Furthermore, throughout this file, you will see at several points text showing in Dark Mint color; note that this is automatically calculated and cannot be changed. 

For guidance on how to correctly complete the ADJ, please click on the icons and refer to the training guides available: 

Guidance Document 

Naming the file: 

Training Video Training Document Q&As 
When it comes to under which name the file shall be saved, note that the file name will need to begin with the letters ADJ, followed by anything else. You can include 
descriptive information, such as 
-for Auditors, the BOS Contract ID, the Job Number, the visit type etc., 
-for Sales, the Company Name, Opportunity Type, etc. 
as there is not limitation on this. 
But, you DEFINITELY need to use the ADJ letters as a prefix For example the file can be stored under the name structure since it starts with 'ADJ': 

ADJ_[BOSContractID]_[JobNo]_[VisitType].xlsx 


Mandatory! 

Storing the file: 

Once completed, 

-Sales need to upload in the Opportunity's respective folder in CS20/Prospects. 

-Auditors must upload the xls file as Attachment in LAD in order to be used on the next visit when needed. By doing so, it will be automatically saved in CS20 upon 
completion of the job in the respective Job folder. 

Editing the file: 

The present is a controlled document and therefore no local changes shall be made or any formulas/macros altered, as this will result in challenges in calculations and the 
team supporting this initiative in not being able to provide technical support. But, most importantly, modifications could also be a potential accreditation risk: this is a global 
tool, so using different/altered versions could lead to findings from our accreditors.

Multi-Site -Notes / Useful Tips 

For further information on definitions etc, see MD1. For further information on determining which Methodology to apply, please refer to MD1 / Sections 6.1, 6.2 and 6.3 

IAF MD1 Issue 3 

In case of a multi-site certification, there are 3 Methodologies that can be applied: 

-MD1, Section 6.1: Methodology for Auditing of a Multi-site Organization Using Site Sampling 
Conditions: Sampling of a set of sites is permitted where the sites are each performing very similar processes/activities (e.g. all sites are Sales/Retail sites). Note that not all organizations 

fulfilling thedefinitionof “multi-site organization”willbe eligible for sampling. 


Not all management systems standards are suitable for consideration for multi-site certification. For example, multi-site sampling would be unsuitable where the audit of variable local 
factors is a requirement of the standard. Specific rules also apply for some schemes, for example those including aerospace (AS 9100 series) or automotive (IATF 16949) and the 
requirements of such schemes shall take precedence. 

When selecting the sites to be visited, consider all possible aspects. 

Size of Sample: The minimum number of sites to be audited per audit is: 
Initial audit:thesize of thesample shallbe thesquare root of the number of sites:(y=√x), roundedup to thenext whole number,where y=number of sites to be sampledandx=total 
number of sites. 
Surveillance audit:thesize of theannual sample shallbe thesquare root of the number of sites with 0.6asacoefficient (y=0.6√x), roundedup to thenext whole number. 
Certificate Renewal/Re-certification audit: the size of the sample shall be the same as for an initial audit. Nevertheless, where the management system has proved to be effective over the 

certificationcycle, thesize of thesample could be reducedto,y=0.8√x,roundedup to thenext whole number. 


In case -during the risk analysis of the process/activity covered by the management system subject to certification -indicates special circumstances, the size or frequency of the sample 
shall be increased. 

-MD1, Section 6.2: Methodology for Auditing of Multi-site Organizations Where Site Sampling Using Section 6.1 is not Appropriate 
In this methodology, all sites need to be visited per specific visit types. 
The audit programme shall consist of an initial audit and recertification audit of all sites. In surveillance audits, 30% of sites, rounded up to the whole number, shall be covered in a calendar 
year. The sites selected for the second surveillance audit will normally be different from the sites selected for the first surveillance audit, leading to all sites being visited at least once 
throughout the certification cycle. 

The audit programme shall be designed to ensure that all processes covered by the certification scope are audited over each cycle. 

-

MD1, Section 6.3: Methodology for Auditing Multi-site Organizations that Include a Combination of Sites that can be Sampled and Other Sites that Cannot be Sampled 

The audit programme shall be established using Section 6.1 for those sites that can be sampled and Section 6.2 for the remaining part of the organization where Section 6.1 is not 
appropriate. 

Note that, regardless of which Methodology is used, the Main Site needs to be visited during the initial certification, re-certification visits and as a minimum once per calendar 
year.
